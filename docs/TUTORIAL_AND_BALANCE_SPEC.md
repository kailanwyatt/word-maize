# Tutorial, education, and balance specification

Status: authoritative MVP onboarding and economy policy

## Teaching principles

- Teach a mechanic only when it first appears on the board.
- Introduce one new obstacle family at a time; combinations are reserved for mastery levels.
- Explain the action the player should take, not only the rule that blocks them.
- Show corn facts as optional-feeling `FIELD NOTE` rewards inside the normal level introduction.
- Keep every level completable without a tool, purchase, advertisement, or prior factual knowledge.

## Educational corn notes

The first level for each corn family includes one concise field note. Blue Corn is explicitly described as naturally dark indigo-purple or nearly black because of anthocyanin pigments. The facts never alter rules or gate progression.

## Difficulty curve

- Special corn begins with two or fewer affected kernels (one for White Corn).
- First levels for Flint, Popcorn, Blue, and Golden Corn use a five-letter-or-lower secondary word goal.
- Introductory harvest targets are eased to 58%, 58%, 60%, and 62% respectively.
- Mechanic density rises gradually inside each variety band.
- Dual-obstacle boards are late mastery challenges, not introduction levels.

## Economy policy

- Energy: five maximum, one campaign attempt, one energy restored every 20 minutes. Resuming a saved run is free.
- Starting tools: two Scarecrows, two Butter Brushes, and one Corn Picker.
- Accepted words pay more for length without making three-letter farming dominant.
- A first clear grants the authored level reward plus a modest performance bonus; replaying pays word coins only.
- Individual coin tools cost 220 / 280 / 340 coins. The 720-coin Field Belt gives a deliberate bundle discount.
- Daily rewards and restoration milestones provide a predictable non-paying supply of tools.
- Real-money bundles accelerate restocking but are never required to complete a level.

## Automated gates

Tests verify introduction levels, tutorial/tool alignment, corn facts, forgiving variety openings, reward bands, tool prices, and obstacle sequencing in addition to existing board validation.
