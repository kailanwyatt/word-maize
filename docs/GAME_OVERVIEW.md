# Word Maize — Game Overview

Status: authoritative description of the current MVP

## The game

Word Maize is a warm, tactile word puzzle about bringing a farming valley back to life. Each level presents a cylindrical cob covered in lettered kernels. The player turns the cob to inspect it, taps any visible kernels in spelling order, and presses the word display to submit. A valid word harvests its kernels into a basket and leaves dimensional sockets or reveals deeper kernels.

The central pleasure is the combination of word discovery, physical-feeling cob rotation, and visible harvesting. It is calm and thoughtful rather than timed by default.

## Player promise

- Find satisfying words without adjacency restrictions.
- Rotate one continuous board instead of playing on a flat letter grid.
- See every successful word physically change the cob.
- Learn one readable farm mechanic at a time.
- Restore fields and landmarks across a storybook farm map.
- Keep playing after the campaign through Endless Harvest.

## Core pillars

1. **Words first.** Obstacles change letter choices but never replace the word puzzle.
2. **Tactile harvest.** Kernels glow when selected, fly into the basket when accepted, and expose matching sockets.
3. **Readable depth.** Native letters and controls sit above reusable transparent artwork. Perspective comes from layout, scale, shade, and tilt.
4. **Fair challenge.** Campaign pressure advances by accepted turns or actions, not by punishing thinking time.
5. **Visible restoration.** Stars and completed levels reopen paths and restore the valley.

## Modes

### Campaign

Sixty authored levels across four farm worlds. Levels introduce rotation, layers, tools, obstacles, weather, and six corn varieties. A player earns up to three stars, coins, story progress, and restoration progress.

### Endless Harvest

Unlocked by completing Level 60. A saved seed and stage generate a reproducible sequence from validated campaign templates. Corn variety cycles every cob, goals rise every three cobs, rewards are paid after each completion, and the next cob opens automatically. A run can be left and continued later; its best completed stage is saved locally.

### Daily Harvest

A seven-day local reward streak that grants coins or tools. It supports retention without changing the word rules.

## Story

The player is a new Word Harvester helping Patch, an earnest young scarecrow, and Farmer May restore a weakened valley before the Harvest Festival. The first farm wakes, animal trouble spreads through Crow Creek, weeds and weather isolate Orchard Hollow, and the communities unite for the final night harvest in Moonlight Maize.

Story is delivered through concise world openings, mechanic introductions, restoration milestones, and world endings. It does not interrupt every level.

## Audience and tone

The intended audience is casual puzzle players and families who enjoy word search, collection, and visual progression. The tone is colorful, premium, encouraging, lightly humorous, and never punitive. Tools are framed as help, not as proof the player failed.

## MVP boundaries

Included: local saves, 60 levels, maps, six corn varieties, obstacles, weather, three tools, coins, energy, daily rewards, store UI, RevenueCat/AdMob adapters, settings, audio, haptics, and Endless Harvest.

Not included: accounts, cloud sync, multiplayer, leaderboards, live-ops backend, competitive timers, user-generated levels, or server-authoritative economy.
